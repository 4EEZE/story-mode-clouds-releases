# Elsewhen wiki

How to change what the mod shows and says with your own resource packs. Everything here is a plain resource pack:
no code, no datapack, reloaded with **F3+T**.

> The memory screen (notes, ideas, research, concepts) came in 0.8.0 "Cranberry" as a proof of concept. Its
> file formats may still change.

- [Resource packs](Resource-packs.md) -- where files go and which pack wins
- Memory screen
  - [Kinds of note](Memory-kinds.md) -- add a kind, recolour one, put it on a layer
  - [Ideas](Ideas.md) -- hide things from ideas, name them
  - [Research](Research.md) -- which creatures, biomes and blocks are noted, and what is said of them
  - [Concepts](Concepts.md) -- ways of making things and their stations (the galaxies)
- [Inner voice](Inner-voice.md) -- its lines, in any language
- [Biome colours](Biome-colours.md) -- fog and sky by biome, and the clouds' shape
