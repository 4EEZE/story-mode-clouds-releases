# story-mode-clouds-releases

Releases of Story Mode Clouds, a NeoForge 1.21.1 client mod: Story Mode style clouds and the light, haze and sounds around them, an inner voice that thinks at quiet moments and remembers your worlds, your friends and your old tools, and the old consoles' menu sounds.

Download the jar from the [latest release](https://github.com/4EEZE/story-mode-clouds-releases/releases/latest) and put it in your `mods` folder. Client only; any server will take you. After that the mod offers each new version on the title screen.
