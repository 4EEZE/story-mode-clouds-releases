# Changelog

Elsewhen (Story Mode Clouds until 0.8.0). Each minor version has a code name; its fix releases follow it.

## 0.8.0 "Cranberry"

> [!CAUTION]
> **THE MEMORY SCREEN IS A PROOF OF CONCEPT. IT HAS NOT BEEN TESTED. TRY IT IF YOU LIKE.**

- The mod is now called Elsewhen. Its files, config and packs keep their old names.
- The memory screen (N): your notes as a map, like a graph in Obsidian, in a copper-black deep. It opens only where it is calm: half a minute without harm, not burning, drowning or freezing, neither too hot nor too cold. It comes as sleep and goes as waking; the world behind it sounds as if from inside a barrel, and the music waits.
- Right click on the dark writes a thought, on a note rewrites it. Hold the right button on a note and it greys and begins to be forgotten: it fades over ten minutes of play, can be held on to while it fades, and then comes back over a few hours.
- Loved places: after a few visits to a quiet place the voice asks whether to remember it (hold the chat key to say yes) and keeps the view from there.
- Ideas: once about half of what a thing is made of has been in your hands, an idea of it comes; once all of it has, the idea is understood. JEI and EMI show only what you know or have held. Turned off under Memories → Ideas.
- Concepts: ways of making things (the crafting table, the furnace, Create's wheels) are galaxies with their ideas round them, found when you hold their station. Things made alike gather into families that open as folders. An idea's recipe is drawn on its card, with fluids, chances and Create's sequenced assembly step by step.
- Research: creatures first met and first beaten, biomes first come to and ores first dug are written down by themselves, on the layer below.
- Layers: Q and E, the number keys, or the wheel past its end take you to another layer.
- Built in for the pack: concepts, families and words about creatures and places for Create, Farmer's Delight, Brewin' and Chewin', Caverns & Chasms, No Man's Land, Enderscape, Darker Depths, Supplementaries, Another Furniture and more.
- All of it can be changed with your own resource packs; the wiki says how.

## 0.7.0 "Old Friends"

- Your worlds remember you. A year to the day since you first came to a world or a server, the voice marks it; and round days too: the 100th, the 200th, a whole year, the 500th, the 1000th.
- Old friends: on a server the voice remembers who you play with, when you met and how long you have spent near each other. It greets a friend not seen for two weeks, and marks years of friendship and hours together.
- Old tools: a tool or weapon long in your hand is noticed after ten, fifty and a hundred hours, and seen off when it breaks after long service, or if you gave it a name.
- A colour of the day: each real day the light has its own faint shade, a little different for every player.
- The title screen remembers the indie years: splashes in your own language, and a birthday wish for the game on 17 May.
- The menus sound as they did on the old consoles: their click, a tick as you move over buttons, steps on sliders, a sound for going back, clicks on inventory slots and a pick-up as you craft. After Console Sounds by Hat. The volume is under Memories; 0 brings back the usual click.
- Render scale: the smaller world is enlarged with sharp pixels, the dither kept in neat two-pixel cells at any scale, and while it is scaled it can wear an old screen's palette (Palette, under World rendering).
- The settings' sidebar scrolls when the sections do not fit, and the reset button is a square arrow you can see.
- Light through windows is gone for now: the sunbeams, the patches of light on the floor and the dust in them cost too many frames. The warm glow of lamps and the light out of lit windows at night stay.
- The five fast settings are off again and the full look is back. If 0.6.5 switched them on for you, they are switched off once; they are still under General.
- Chat works alongside other chat mods, and the Russian thoughts no longer assume who you are.

### 0.7.1

- Fixed a crash near redstone from other mods: Caverns & Chasms’ refractor, and any block like it, no longer stops the game when the inner voice looks for redstone at work.

## 0.6.0 "Mist & Moonlight"

- Air: a haze that thickens with distance and lies low in the valleys, in the colours of the fog and the sky, glowing toward a low sun. Mist gathers before sunrise, the air thickens in rain under open sky, and swamps, jungles and other foggy biomes are hazier.
- Biome colours from Kev's Earth & Sky, built in: soft pastel fog and sky for 63 biomes, and air thicker in forests, mountains and deserts. The resource pack is no longer needed.
- Caves have a dark fog of their own that closes in around you, and the world eases back slowly as you come out. No more grey sky and black clouds at a tunnel's mouth, nor white sky where a cave runs on out of sight.
- Sun and moon rays after Sunbathing, built in: they glow through thin cloud, stay behind the hills the horizon dissolves, and the moon wears a small blue halo. The resource pack is no longer needed.
- Light through windows: sunbeams and moonbeams through glass at the sun's true slope, dust drifting in them, and sunlit patches on the floor and walls in the window's own shape, soft and grainy the further they fall. Stained, tinted and mosaic glass colour the light; frames, bars and curtains cast their pattern. Another Furniture's curtains dim and colour the light when drawn, and closed shutters let it through their gaps alone. Create's windows, joined ones too, let their light through. An open glass door, or a curtain with no glass behind it, is an opening, not a window.
- Warm light at night: the air round torches, lanterns and fires glows softly, each flickering its own way -- a campfire lively, a candle barely, a lantern steady -- and a lit room's light spills out of its windows into the night. Lamps on Sable's ships glow as they sail.
- Nights are dark: caves, unlit rooms and moonless fields stay dark whatever the Brightness setting, moonlight is faint and blue and darker still as the moon wanes, and moonlit shafts are gentle. Low sun warms the light at golden hour; mornings are paler and fresher than evenings.
- Colour: warmer whites, dusky violet shadows, cream highlights and a soft vignette, shifting with where you are: deeper and darker in caves, cooler in rain and at night, warmer in warm biomes. Stepping in from the dark to a lit room warms it like shelter, and after rain the day looks fresh for a while.
- Small moments: at home in a lit room on a dark or rainy night, the weather is heard at the window now and then -- a gust, a tap at the frame, raindrops on the glass.
- An inner voice: now and then a thought of your own, written out over the hotbar on a soft haze that dissolves in the horizon's grain, the words floating a little and drifting apart as the thought goes. Each player's voice is their own, a little higher or lower, quicker or slower; soft for a skin with slim arms, rough for wide ones; slower, lower and gloomier when hurt. At bedtime it looks back on the day. It speaks up on entering a world or joining a server, on waking, at sunrise and sunset, under the stars, as rain or a storm begins and as the rain stops, in falling snow, when you are hungry, too hot or cold (Cold Sweat's temperature if it is there), after a good meal such as Farmer's Delight's, underground, coming in from the dark to your lit home -- home being where you sleep most nights; another lit room is only a shelter, and your bed out in the open a camp -- by a campfire, cooking with Farmer's Delight, among Create's machines or on its trains, by redstone at work, tipsy with Brewin' and Chewin', fishing, looking at a painting, riding, with your pet close by, among villagers, high up with a view, when a record plays, when your pockets are full, running out of air, on fire, after a long fall, badly hurt, back from death, at every tenth level, in the Nether, the End and back again, and past midnight by the real clock; now and then a stray thought comes for no reason, a joke or a memory. The more often a moment comes the rarer its thought, and each has its own chance in the settings. Over 1300 lines in English and in Russian.
- A warm moon, golden cream instead of cold white, after Yamashita Shimejize.
- A new settings screen on K: colour-coded sections, search, and a live preview you can show at dawn, noon, dusk, night or in rain. Hold "before" to compare with how it was.
- A load panel on F10: what the mod costs, pass by pass.
- Full Russian translation.
- Removed the low torn clouds that hung on after rain.
- No Man's Land: no more clouds in the moon friend's dream, and its fog gives way to the haze and the cave fog.
- Fixed OpenGL errors flooding the log at the edge of the world.
- Fixed Cold Sweat's heat blur stacking dozens of times over after respawns and dimension changes, and another player's heatstroke flashing on your screen when they join.

### 0.6.1

- Much less lag in the Nether and by lava lakes: lava is no longer one of the warm glow's lamps -- a lava sea was thousands of them, sorted many times a second.
- No more holes of bare colour through a Nether portal: where there is no sky, the edge of the world is no longer dissolved; the Nether's own fog hides it.
- The haze works alongside Veil, which comes with Sable: its shader no longer fails to compile there.
- Light through windows: the blurred edges of a sunlit patch break up in the horizon's ordered dither rather than two patterns at odds, and a patch is never brighter than the same surface under the open sky.
- Inner voice: Cold Sweat's temperature is read at last -- the voice looked for it under the wrong name -- and heat and cold are felt after five seconds rather than ten.
- Updates: the mod looks for a new version when the game starts and offers it on the title screen; it is downloaded, checked and put in place when you close the game. Silent updates, without asking, can be turned on in the settings under Updates.

### 0.6.2

- Inner voice: thoughts for coming back under the sky after a long while underground, a long swim, gliding on elytra, holding a map, reading a book, an enchanting table, a trade, the chime of amethyst, a bell, a first diamond in your pockets, and advancements -- the challenges with their own. A chime or a closed book waits for its turn instead of being lost, no thought starts over an open screen, and the last lines heard are not the first ones again next time.

### 0.6.3

- Faster: the warm glow picks its lamps without sorting every glowing block round you, and blocks changed by a contraption, a ship or a felled tree are taken a little at a time rather than in one stalled frame. Shafts above their clouds and the haze's light do less needless work, and two new settings, Air Light Samples and Cloud Pixel Sample Divisor, trade a little detail for speed.
- Fixed a rare crash with AsyncParticles: the dust in sunbeams used one random from two threads.
- Inner voice: it remembers where you died and the bed of your first night, and thinks of them when you come back from afar; on a server, it notices another player come close after a while alone. On a server with no one else online, it has thoughts of its own for the quiet; and not every player is a friend: one who kills you, or keeps hitting you, is met warily for a week after. New thoughts for Exposure's photographs and albums and the Chimes mod's wind chimes; Scholar's books count as books; a nap in a Sleep Tight hammock is no end of the day.

### 0.6.4

- Light through windows: beams use fewer samples when nearby geometry stops them, keeping detail near the camera. At the test window, the same-run A/B march fell from 0.850 to 0.260 ms per frame; the small visual change was accepted.
- Inner voice: a neutral thought in the selected language now comes before an English mood fallback. Damaged or blank resource-pack lines are skipped, and Stray Thought Chance has its own setting.
- Memories of beds, death places and rivals ignore damaged entries; memory files are replaced after being fully written. Turning the voice off clears waiting thoughts.
- Thought glyphs are laid out once per phrase, and full Unicode characters appear together. Shelter checks skip sky work when no warmth can show.

### 0.6.5

- Five fast settings now favor frame rate by default. They keep the main cloud layer and skip the horizon dissolve and atmosphere, screen-space light, cloud shafts, shadows and colour grade, and two background layers. Each group can be restored in General settings.
- Unused cloud coverage uploads, light-volume tracking and sun-dust searches are skipped. Cloud tint changes no longer rebuild a stationary mesh. F10 now shows world rendering stages and client tick costs.
- The sleep veil eases in on beds with long sleep timers. New thoughts remember old worlds, the road home, hard nights and quiet places. The first shelter is remembered only after the player moves away.
- Ordinary chat has a soft panel, player colours and positional voices for nearby players. Punctuation shapes the delivery; chat appearance and voice have their own settings.
- New contextual voice lines. Fixed a Cold Sweat callback crash, a lightmap adjustment left on by the master switch, and sun-dust counts after world changes.
